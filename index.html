Backend Developer – Math Expertise

Remote Utrecht / NL preferred Internship · Thesis · Contractor

The role

You will work directly alongside the founder — a 26-year math teaching expert — to develop and stabilise the core math logic of our backend, used for plotting functions, solving equations, and evaluating functions, derivatives, and definite integrals. You will help debug, harden the parsing pipeline, handle edge cases, and contribute to the mathematical logic that makes the product work. This is not a support role — you will be close to the technical heart of the product.

You're a great fit if you

  • Have degree-level mathematics — calculus is essential
  • Are proficient in JavaScript / Node.js and comfortable with backend logic
  • Have hands-on experience with computational math — parsing, evaluating, or manipulating math expressions programmatically
  • Can work independently from partial documentation in an early-stage codebase
  • Are a student or recent graduate open to an internship or external thesis placement (internship allowance provided); or a contractor open to a mix of small cash and deferred pay

An exceptional candidate with relevant hands-on experience may also receive a small, milestone-vested equity stake.
